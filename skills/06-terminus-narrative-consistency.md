# Terminus Narrative Consistency

Protect Terminus' grounded post-collapse tone and ensure narrative claims correspond to gameplay.

## Tone
Terminus should feel:
- grounded
- tense
- human
- practical
- bleak without being melodramatic
- post-collapse rather than superheroic

Avoid generic videogame language.

Prefer:
"We've got movement south of the old depot."

over:
"A new enemy wave has spawned."

## Gameplay truth
Narrative must not promise mechanics that do not exist.

If a transmission says:
- raiders are approaching
- infected activity will intensify tonight
- a settlement is under attack
- someone has contacted the player
- a location contains something specific

then the game should actually support that claim.

Otherwise implement the missing hook or rewrite the text.

## Mission writing
Every mission should answer:
- Who is speaking?
- Why are they contacting the player?
- What do they know?
- Why does the player care?
- What does accepting mean?
- What does declining mean?
- What actually happens in gameplay?
- What are the consequences?

## Branching
Where appropriate, choices should alter:
- objectives
- rewards
- faction standing
- narrative flags
- future mission availability

Avoid cosmetic choices masquerading as meaningful branches.

## Sandbox
Campaign content should create direction and urgency without turning Terminus into a linear rail shooter. The simulation should continue independently of the story.
